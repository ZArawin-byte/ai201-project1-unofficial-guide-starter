Hot take: do not bring a car your first year. Here's the math.

Student parking permits are sold by lottery, not first-come. There are about 1,200 permits for way more applicants, so plenty of people pay the $480/year and still don't win a spot in the central lots — they get stuck in the Outer West lot, which is a 15-minute walk from the academic core anyway. You might as well not drive.

reply ↳ u/shuttle_stan (140 points)
The Gold Line shuttle is genuinely good and it's FREE with your student ID. It loops from the dorms through the academic core to the off-campus apartment district every 10 minutes during the day, every 20 minutes after 7 PM. Download the LakeTransit app — it has live GPS tracking so you're not standing in the cold guessing.

reply ↳ u/bike_commuter (76 points)
Biking is the fastest way across campus, full stop. Register your bike with campus security (free) or it can get impounded from the racks during break cleanups. There are covered, locked bike cages near each dorm cluster — you need to opt in at the housing office to get cage access.

reply ↳ u/townie22 (31 points)
If you DO need a car occasionally, there's a Zipcar pod by the Union. Cheaper than the permit if you only drive a few times a month for grocery runs.
