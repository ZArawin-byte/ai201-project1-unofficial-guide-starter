== Dining Hall Showdown: where to eat and when ==

Northgate Commons is the biggest dining hall and the one everyone defaults to, which is exactly why you should avoid it between 12:00 and 1:00 PM. The lunch line wraps around the salad bar and you'll wait 20-25 minutes minimum. The food is fine — standard rotating stations — but the wait is brutal at peak. Go before 11:45 or after 1:15 and it's a totally different experience.

Hillcrest Market is the move if you're short on time. It's grab-and-go style: pre-made sandwiches, wraps, a hot bar you serve yourself, and self-checkout. Almost no wait even at noon. The tradeoff is less variety and it closes early (8:00 PM on weekdays). Most people don't realize Hillcrest takes a regular meal swipe, not just dining dollars.

The Lakeside Cafe has the best actual food quality — made-to-order pasta and a rotating chef special — but it's small, gets loud, and the made-to-order line is slow. Worth it for dinner, not for a quick lunch.

&nbsp;

Meal plan tip: unused meal swipes do NOT roll over week to week, but dining dollars DO roll over to the end of the semester. If you have extra swipes near the end of a week, use them at Hillcrest to grab packaged snacks for later. Burning a swipe on a $4 sandwich beats losing it.

Weekend hours are reduced — Northgate is the only hall open continuously on Saturday and Sunday; Hillcrest and Lakeside are closed Sundays.
