The Student Health Center is free for enrolled students — it's already covered by your health fee, so don't skip it thinking you'll get a bill. Walk-ins exist but the wait can hit two hours midday; booking online the night before gets you a same-day slot with almost no wait. Most students don't know online booking exists.

Counseling (CAPS) is the service people most need and most misunderstand. The free counseling sessions are capped per semester, and during midterms and finals the wait for a first appointment stretches to two or three weeks. The workaround: CAPS runs same-day crisis drop-in hours every weekday afternoon, and they have a 24/7 phone line. If you're in a bad spot, you do not have to wait three weeks — use the drop-in.

The Rec Center (the campus gym) is free with your student ID. Weekday hours are 6 AM to 11 PM. Weekend hours are shorter and this trips people up — it closes at 8 PM on Saturdays and isn't open at all before noon on Sundays. The climbing wall and the pool have separate, shorter hours posted at the desk, not online.

Flu shots are free at the Health Center every fall — they run a walk-in clinic in the Union for the first two weeks of October, no appointment. Worth it; flu rips through the dorms every year.

Pharmacy: there's a small one inside the Health Center for prescriptions, but it closes at 5 PM and is closed weekends. For anything urgent on a weekend you're going to the 24-hour pharmacy off campus on Route 9.
