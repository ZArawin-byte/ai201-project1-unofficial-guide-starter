Textbooks first because that's where they get you. NEVER buy from the campus bookstore at list price. In order of cheapest: (1) the Carver Library Reserve Desk lends the required textbook for most large intro courses for free — 2-hour in-library loans, and you can renew if nobody's waiting. (2) Rent used from online sellers. (3) Buy used from the upperclassmen Facebook group. The bookstore should be your absolute last resort.

reply ↳ u/free_food_finder (134 points):
Follow @LakemontFreeFood — it's a student-run account that posts whenever a department event has leftover catering. Free pizza and sandwiches almost daily if you move fast. Club info sessions in the first three weeks of the semester are basically a free meal tour.

reply ↳ u/discount_hunter (88 points):
Your student ID is a discount card most people never use. Local bus is free with it, the downtown movie theater does $6 student Tuesdays, and a bunch of Route 9 restaurants do 10-15% student discounts if you just ask — they don't advertise it.

reply ↳ u/laundry_quarters (45 points):
Laundry is included in dorm housing fees now, so that's not a cost if you live on campus. Off campus, the laundromat on Westbrook Ave does a student wash-dry-fold deal on weekdays.

reply ↳ u/printing_tip (29 points):
Every student gets a $30 printing credit per semester loaded onto their ID automatically. Most people forget and it doesn't roll over. Use it before finals.
