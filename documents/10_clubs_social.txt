Making Friends and Finding Your People at Lakemont
Student Life Blog  |  by a graduating senior  |  Share  |  Comments (61)

The thing nobody tells freshmen: the friend groups that last form in the first six weeks, and almost all of them form through repeated low-stakes contact, not big events. Join something that meets weekly. A club that meets once is forgettable; a club that meets every Tuesday becomes your people by October.

The Involvement Fair happens the second week of the semester on the Union lawn — over 200 clubs table there. It's overwhelming. Strategy: don't sign up for fifteen clubs, you won't go to any of them. Pick two or three, actually show up to those.

Intramural sports are the most underrated social entry point. You do not need to be good. Rec-level intramural soccer, volleyball, and "innertube water polo" (yes, that's real here) are full of people who just want a low-pressure weekly hang. Sign up as a free agent if you don't have a team and they'll place you on one.

A warning about Greek life: it's about 12% of campus here, smaller than at a lot of schools, and recruitment is in the spring not the fall — so don't feel like you have to figure it out in week one. You have a whole semester first.

Commuter and transfer students: the Commuter Lounge in the Union basement is the hub. Transfer-specific orientation events get buried in email — check the transfer student newsletter, not the general one.
