The Definitive Lakemont Study Spot Guide (updated each semester)
Share on Twitter | Share on Facebook | Print

If you want SILENCE, go to the 4th floor of Carver Library. It's a designated silent floor — no talking, no phone calls, and people will absolutely shush you. Individual carrels with outlets at every seat. This is where you go during finals when you actually need to lock in.

Do NOT try to study quietly on the 1st floor of Carver. The first floor is the social floor — group work, coffee cart, constant chatter. Great for group projects, terrible for focused solo work. People confuse the two floors all the time and then complain.

The best-kept secret is the Annex basement (the building behind the Student Union). Almost nobody knows it has study space because there's no signage. Quiet, warm in winter, and it has the only 24-hour study room on campus that doesn't require a keycard after 10 PM.

The Science Building atrium has great natural light and big tables, but it's freezing — they keep it cold for the lab equipment. Bring a jacket. Outlets are scarce; grab a wall seat or bring a battery pack.

Coffee shop options: Grind House (off-campus, 5 min walk) has fast wifi and stays open till midnight. The Union Starbucks closes at 6 PM and the wifi crawls during peak hours because everyone's on it.

Pro tip: during finals week the library extends to 24 hours, but the 4th floor silent rule is enforced even harder — RAs do quiet patrols.
