Lakemont On-Campus Housing — The Honest Dorm Tier List
Navigation: Home > Housing > Reviews        Sign in to comment

Tier S: Riverside Hall. Newest dorm, built 2019. Air conditioning (rare on this campus!), suite-style with a shared bathroom for every 4 people instead of a communal hall bathroom. The catch: it's the farthest dorm from the academic core, a solid 12-minute walk. You trade convenience for comfort.

Tier A: Maple and Birch Halls. Central location, 4 minutes to most classes. NO air conditioning — and Lakemont gets genuinely hot in late August and September. Buy a fan before you arrive; the campus store sells out the first week every single year. Hall-style bathrooms but they're cleaned daily.

Tier C: Old Quad (Foster, Pierce, and Adams Halls). These are the historic dorms. Charming from the outside, rough on the inside: radiator heat you can't control (it's either off or sauna), thin walls, and laundry in the basement with only 4 machines for the whole building. Cheapest option though, and the rooms are bigger than the new dorms.

&amp;nbsp;

Laundry tip that applies everywhere: the LakeWash app shows which machines are free in real time so you don't haul your basket down for nothing. Laundry is included in housing fees — it's not coin-op anymore.

Housing lottery note: the lottery IS random within your class year, but you get a better lottery number if you submit your housing application before the priority deadline. Late applications go to the bottom regardless. So "random" has an asterisk.
