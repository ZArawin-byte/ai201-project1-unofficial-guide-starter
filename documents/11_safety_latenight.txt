The free safe-walk service is the thing to actually remember. Call campus security any time after dark and a student safety escort will walk you anywhere on campus or to the edge of the off-campus apartment district. It's free, it's not judged, and the wait is usually under ten minutes. The number is on the back of your student ID.

There's also a late-night shuttle separate from the daytime Gold Line: the SafeRide van runs 9 PM to 3 AM and does on-demand pickups across campus and the near neighborhoods. Request it through the LakeTransit app. People forget it exists because it's not on the regular route map.

Blue-light emergency phones are spaced across campus — press the button and it connects directly to security with your location. Good to know where the nearest one is on your normal routes, especially the path between the library and the Outer West parking lot, which is poorly lit.

Practical stuff: the Annex basement 24-hour study room is safe and monitored by camera, but the walk there from the dorms after midnight is dark — use SafeRide. Lakemont's an overall low-crime area but bike theft is the one real problem; always lock to the cages, never to a random rack overnight.

Save campus security's number now, before you need it: it's a non-emergency line that's far faster than 911 for on-campus stuff like a lockout, a dead car battery in the lot, or a safe walk.
