What to Actually Bring to Lakemont (and the First-Week Survival List)
Incoming Student Guide • shared 600+ times • Comments

Lakemont weather whiplash is real. Late August and September are hot and humid — you'll be sweating in a dorm with no AC (most dorms don't have it). Then it flips fast: October is gorgeous, November gets cold, and by December there's real snow that stays. Pack for both. The mistake every out-of-state freshman makes is bringing only one season's clothes for move-in.

Must-bring that people forget: a fan (campus store sells out week one — buy before you arrive), a good winter coat that you DON'T wait until November to acquire, shower shoes for hall bathrooms, a surge protector (dorm rooms have maybe two outlets), and rain gear because the walk to class doesn't stop for weather.

First-week logistics: get your student ID photo done before move-in day if they offer it online — the in-person line on move-in day is over an hour. Set up your campus laundry app and meal plan app before you need them. Find your classrooms the day before classes start; the building names and the building codes on your schedule don't always match and people get lost on day one.

Don't overbuy dorm stuff. The "dorm shopping" lists from big retailers are designed to sell you things. You do not need a 12-piece kitchen set in a room with no kitchen. Coordinate with your roommate before move-in so you don't both bring a mini-fridge — one is plenty and they're the single most-duplicated item.

Winter note: classes rarely cancel for snow here. "It's just snow, walk to class" is the official attitude. Boots with actual traction matter more than you think on the hill between the dorms and the science quad.
